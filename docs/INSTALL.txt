SMWaystones - craftable teleport waystones for Scrap Mechanic Survival
======================================================================

Made for Scrap Mechanic 1.0.7a (Steam, Windows 64-bit).

REQUIRES SMLoader 1.1.0 or newer: https://github.com/CazymirTM/SMLoader
CO-OP: EVERY player in the world needs SMLoader + SMWaystones.
       (Co-op has not been tested yet.)

INSTALL
-------
1. Install SMLoader first (see its README).
2. Open your game folder:
   Steam -> right-click Scrap Mechanic -> Manage -> Browse local files.
3. Copy the  SMLoader  folder from this zip into the game folder and allow it
   to merge with the existing SMLoader folder. It adds:
     SMLoader\mods\SMWaystones.dll
4. Load a Survival world and craft a Waystone in any Craftbot.

USE
---
- Craft: 10 Metal Block 1 + 1 Circuit Board (unlocked from the start).
- Place two or more waystones. Press E on one to rename it or to travel.

GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Steam achievements keep working with SMLoader 1.3.0 or newer. With an older
  SMLoader the game stops counting them while the mod is installed.
- Back up your world before trying any mod.

UNINSTALL
---------
Delete  SMLoader\mods\SMWaystones.dll  and the  SMLoader\SMWaystones  folder.
Waystones in your worlds become checkered "missing part" cubes and come back,
with their names, if you reinstall.
